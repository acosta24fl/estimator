# Director's Brief: "Quote it before you leave" (TikTok)

## Film in one line
A handyman finishes looking at a customer's bathroom, pulls out his phone,
taps in the job, and turns the screen around with a real price, right there
on the spot.

## Format
- **Vertical 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 20 s**, made for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- **Safe zones:** no text in the top 160 px or the bottom 420 px (TikTok
  caption + buttons), and nothing important in the right 140 px (like/share
  column).
- **Hook in the first second:** the customer's question is on screen at frame 1.
- Text big enough to read on a phone: headlines ≥ 96 px, UI scaled 2.4×.

## Audience & goal
Handymen and small trades scrolling TikTok. Goal: "that's me", then install.

## Character bible
**The handyman** (unnamed, so viewers see themselves)
- Flat vector illustration, geometric shapes, no outlines, soft shadow
  shapes. Not 3D and not cartoon-goofy.
- Navy cap, navy work shirt with rolled sleeves, tool belt with a hammer,
  short beard. Medium-brown skin tone.
- Calm and competent. He never looks stressed, and his only expressions are
  *thinking* (eyes up, mouth flat) and *confident* (slight smile, one eyebrow up).
- Holds the phone in his left hand and taps with his right thumb.
- Views: 3/4 front (main), plus a close-up of hands + phone.

**The customer** (homeowner)
- Same flat style. Grey sweater, mug in hand. Only seen from the shoulders up,
  at the frame edge.
- Expressions: *asking* (eyebrows up), *pleased* (smile, nod).

**Palette exception:** the brand palette stays the same for all UI, text and
backgrounds. Skin tones, the sweater and the tool-belt brown are used **only on
the characters**.

## Setting
A simple flat bathroom: a tiled wall in a grid pattern (brand surface colours),
a vanity outline and a doorway. It's dark-themed so the brand colours still
dominate.

## Tempo
128 BPM, 4/4, upbeat. 10 bars = 18.75 s + 1.25 s tail = 20 s.

## Beat sheet
| Bars | Time | Shot | What happens | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–1.9 s | **Hook** | Wide: the handyman looks at the cracked tile wall. The customer leans in from the edge with a speech bubble: **"So… how much?"** | Kick + clap |
| 2 | 1.9–3.8 s | **No napkin** | The handyman's *thinking* face, then *confident*. He pulls his phone out of the tool belt on beat 6. Text: **"Quote it on the spot."** | Whoosh on the phone pull |
| 3–5 | 3.8–9.4 s | **Phone close-up** | Push into hands + phone. The real app (mobile layout): his thumb taps, and "Porcelain tile · 4.25 · 60" then "Thinset + grout · 38 · 2" type in on the beat. Materials subtotal climbs **$255 → $331**. | Typing ticks on 16ths, chimes |
| 6 | 9.4–11.3 s | **Labor** | Swipe up on the phone: $45/hr, 10 h. His thumb taps **Generate Estimate** on beat 23.5. | Swipe, ticks, click |
| 7 | 11.3–13.1 s | **The number** | The price rolls up on the phone and lands **$1,360.87** in green. | Roll, chime |
| 8–9 | 13.1–16.9 s | **The turn** | Back to the wide shot: he turns the phone to the customer. Phone shows Budget / Standard / Premium. The customer goes *pleased* and nods; a handshake lands on beat 32. Text: **"Three prices. No guessing."** | Hits, chime on the handshake |
| 10 | 16.9–20 s | **End card** | Icon + **"Quote it before you leave."** + "Free · Works offline". | Final hit, tail |

## On-screen text
1. So… how much? *(speech bubble)*
2. Quote it on the spot.
3. Three prices. No guessing.
4. Quote it before you leave.
5. Free · Works offline

## Must not
- Claim a specific time or show a stopwatch.
- Show any number not from the real app (sample tile job).
- Put text in the TikTok safe-zone margins.
- Make the characters goofy, 3D or "AI-looking". Use flat shapes and calm motion.
